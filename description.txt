[b]All Stats Scaled Suvius'[/b]! This is a revolutionary mod made for your favorite game, [i]Terraria[/i], bringing balance and challenge to the enemies in your world! Say goodbye to static, boring enemy difficulty and welcome fun, customizable stat scaling—[b]you[/b] control how enemies scale in health, damage, and defense to simulate full team combat. [b]Fight or be forgotten![/b]

[b]Features:[/b]
[list]
[*]Enemy health, defense, contact damage, movement speed and invincibility frames
[*]Enemy projectile damage and speed
[*]Simulate player count to scale boss stats (Expert and Master only)
[*]Respawn time, boss fight respawn time, and instant respawn when the boss fight ends (multiplayer)
[*]Player max health, defense, life regen, invincibility frames, knockback taken and speed
[*]Every setting from 1% to 1000%, or type any value
[*]Languages: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Polish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Simplified Chinese
[/list]
